Independent research, 2024–2026

One research programme across inflation forecasting, policy evaluation, global macro, macroeconomic models and research infrastructure

Since May 2024 – present I have taken a career break for family caring responsibilities and used it for one integrated programme of independent research. It runs along the whole chain of applied macroeconomics: measuring prices and activity, forecasting inflation, building and applying macroeconomic models for advanced and emerging economies, evaluating policy, and the research infrastructure that makes every result reproducible and checkable. Each Project note below covers one project: the question, the approach, what the work shows, the insights it gave, and its scope and next steps. Every note follows the same structure and can be downloaded as PDF or Word.

Figures and tables marked simulated are generated from simulated data built to share the structure of the analysis (its variables, horizons and frequencies). They show how each method works and what its output looks like. Results stated in the text, and figures that give a source, are the project’s own.

Inflation forecasting

Forecasting systems for headline, core and component inflation across advanced and emerging economies, built to be judged out of sample against naive and professional benchmarks.

A global underlying-inflation monitor with a blended projection system
One monthly view of where inflation is really heading in 22 economies, with projections that combine model drivers and an outside forecast
Forecasting US core inflation from the bottom up
A component-by-component forecast, with producer prices translated into core PCE, tested against four major banks and the consensus
One engine, many forecasting methods, back-tested across 20 economies
An automated price-forecasting engine that fits several methods per series, combines them, and is back-tested against a naive benchmark
Combining forecasting methods is the robust choice
Across 181 US consumer-price components, the leading method shifts from component to component and month to month, and a simple average of the methods is the most accurate forecast over the full test year
An automated feed for component-level US inflation
A routine that pulls official price data, keeps it current and turns it into monthly contributions to headline inflation
Inflation forecasts as probabilities, not single numbers
A monthly forecast pack for 22 economies that pools independent views of the next print and the year ahead into one distribution, and reads it as the odds of crossing the thresholds that matter

Global macro and emerging markets

Cross-country diagnostics of growth, inflation, policy and external risk, from monitoring notes to long-run panels across advanced and emerging economies.

Global macro monitoring and external-debt vulnerability
A repeatable way to turn a week’s data flow into a causal read of inflation, policy and external risk
Central Europe’s industrial drift from Germany
A gradual decoupling, not a structural break, and a policy rate close to its rule
A cross-country macro panel and what global shocks do to small open economies
An automated monthly and quarterly panel for five small open economies, organised around global shocks, uncertainty and financial vulnerability
What drives industrial output in five emerging economies
Across 27 years of monthly data, global risk appetite, rather than domestic policy rates, is the robust driver of industrial output
Demographics and growth in emerging markets
How demographics and social conditions line up with projected long-run growth across eighteen emerging economies
Was Mexico really more productive than the US in 1980?
A puzzle in the standard cross-country productivity data turns out to be mostly a price-comparison artefact

A working map of macroeconomic model families
From the three-equation model to DSGE, and what each says about exchange rates
Currency divergence scenarios for central Europe
A quarterly-projection model extended with political-credibility and capital-flow risk terms, used to frame how neighbouring currencies could part ways
An AI-assisted workflow for macroeconomic models
Evidence sourced in tiers, dated and logged, and machine-written code audited by tests, so every output can be checked
Derivation notes for the core macro models
Step-by-step algebra from first principles, ending in a stability check a reader can verify by hand
Nowcasting with mixed-frequency data and a shock module

Methods and research infrastructure

Reusable methods and infrastructure for extracting the signal in economic data at scale and making econometric results reproducible and checkable.

Finding the signal in economic data
Reusable tools for removing seasonality, reading trends and measuring how price series move together, frequency by frequency
Signal extraction across tens of thousands of series pairs
A pipeline that decomposes thousands of price and activity series and measures how every pair moves together, without ever repeating a pair
Making econometric results checkable
Machine-written model code is checked against authoritative references before it runs, and results are regenerated exactly from stored inputs.
A pre-registered framework for testing trading rules
A net-of-cost, out-of-sample design, corrected for multiple testing, that separates genuine predictive power from data-mined results, applied to a time-varying-sensitivity rule and a calendar rule
Do energy-price surprises cross the US–Mexico border?
A chance benchmark and a like-for-like comparison turn a screen of tens of thousands of price-series pairs into a test
